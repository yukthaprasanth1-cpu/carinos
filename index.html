Dear Carino, ♡

There are so many little things about us that stay in my mind, even the ones that probably seem completely ordinary.

I love that we have spent time together with your family and with my brothers. I love the days when we watched movies together, and especially those nights when somehow we would start talking and suddenly it was already two or three in the morning. I don't even know how the hours disappeared so quickly.

I loved cooking together too. Honestly, half the time I wasn't even concentrating on what we were cooking because I was too busy watching you. I would end up taking videos of you because I genuinely love taking videos of you. And you never really let me do much work while we were cooking anyway.

You used to be there whenever I needed you, except for those times when you were busy, and that meant more to me than I probably said out loud.

And then there were the flowers.

Whenever you went somewhere and got flowers, you would get them for me. And if you happened to see flowers somewhere, you would sometimes just pick one and give it to me.

Even those tiny little sunflower-looking flowers make me smile because you would pick six or seven of them and make this tiny little bouquet.

I absolutely adore hearing you sing. You could sing the same song a million times and I would still want to hear you sing it again. There is just something about hearing your voice that I love, and I don't think I could ever get tired of it.

And I can never forget the first bouquet you gave me.

It had yellow flowers, purple flowers whose name I still don't know, a pink rose, and hibiscus. It was the first bouquet and the first flowers I received from you, so it became special to me in a way that probably had nothing to do with the flowers themselves.

It's funny because some of my favourite memories aren't even huge moments. They're the little things. Cooking. Singing. Watching movies. Staying awake way too late. You bringing me flowers. Me watching you while you're doing something and secretly taking another video.

And yes… after all this time, I still remember the cheesecake. I asked for cheesecake for FOUR MONTHS and somehow I still haven't received it. 😭

But jokes aside, no matter who you become, or how much you change, I know there will always be a part of me that cares about you and adores you.

I hope you know that you are loved. I hope you know that you matter. And I hope you know that all those tiny memories we made have a special place in my heart.

Happy birthday, Carino. ♡

I am STILL waiting for my cheesecake. 😭🤍
