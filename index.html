Dear Carino ♡

There are so many little things about us that I keep thinking about. Not just the huge moments, but the tiny ordinary ones that somehow became some of the most special memories for me.

I loved all the time we spent together — being around your family, spending time with my brothers, watching movies together, and just being around each other. Even doing absolutely nothing somehow felt special when it was with you.

And then there were those nights when we would stay up talking until two or three in the morning. We would keep talking and talking, even when we both knew we probably should have been sleeping. Somehow those late-night conversations became some of my favourite memories.

I also loved cooking together. You probably don't even realise how much I enjoyed those moments. I would usually end up watching you more than actually helping, and of course I would take videos of you because I genuinely love taking videos of you.

And you never really let me do much work while we were cooking. You would just do things yourself and somehow make it feel like I was there to keep you company rather than actually help.

I remember how you used to be there whenever I needed you, except for those times when you were genuinely busy. It meant a lot to me knowing that I could look for you and you'd be there.

And the flowers… I don't think you ever realised how much those tiny gestures meant to me. Whenever you went somewhere and got me flowers, I would be so happy. Even if you simply saw flowers somewhere and picked one for me, it meant so much.

I especially remember those tiny sunflower-like flowers. You would pick six or seven of them and make this tiny little bouquet. It wasn't some huge expensive thing, but somehow that made it even more special to me.

And I absolutely adore hearing you sing. You could sing the same song a million times and I would still want to hear you sing it again. There is just something about hearing you sing that makes me ridiculously happy.

And then there was your first bouquet for me. The yellow flowers, the purple flowers whose name I still don't know, the pink rose, and the hibiscus. I still remember it because it was the first bouquet of flowers I received from you, and that makes it special in a way that another bouquet could never completely replace.

There is also one very important thing that I absolutely cannot forget…

I asked you for cheesecake for FOUR MONTHS. 😭

And somehow I am still waiting for it. FOUR MONTHS, Carino. FOUR. MONTHS. 😭

But honestly, jokes aside, there is something I want you to know. No matter who you become, or how much you change, or who you are at any point in your life, I will still care about you. I will still adore the little things about you that made these memories so special to me.

These memories will always have a special place in my heart. The flowers, the late nights, the movies, cooking together, singing, the random videos, the little bouquets, and all those ordinary moments that became extraordinary simply because they happened with you.

I hope you know how loved you are, how much you matter, and how many little pieces of happiness you have brought into my life.

Happy birthday, Carino. ♡

And yes… I am STILL waiting for my cheesecake. 😭🤍

With love, always ♡
